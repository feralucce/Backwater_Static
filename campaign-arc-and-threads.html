Campaign Arc & Threads

GM Reference · Chapter 09
Everything on this page is spoilers

What the season is actually building toward, split explicitly into decided threads and genuinely open ones, so nothing here gets mistaken for more settled than it is.

Thread: Marcus's Fate

Three branches, not three simultaneous truths - pick one for your table, or reveal ambiguously early and let it stay genuinely unresolved for a while.

  • A. Recovered

    In the chaos of the Session 1 retreat, a Foundation element recovered his unconscious body. Site 23 holds him now, quietly, unpublicized, because he's living evidence of what the Incident was riding. "Find Marcus" becomes "find which Foundation facility has him," building toward Site 23 or Repository 23 as a real target later in the season.

  • B. Still Ridden

    The Flash's exit was theater as much as truth - the Incident loves a clean, dramatic break better than a slow fade. It kept a thread in him and took him bodily when it fled the scene, wearing him somewhere outside the city for whatever chaos it's building toward next. Finding Marcus means finding the Incident, and getting him back means fighting for a body that's still occupied.

  • C. Displaced

    The Incident genuinely left him, but the leaving wasn't clean. Being host to a bodyless entity, then having it torn free in one Flash, knocked something in him out of alignment - he's free of it, but somewhere he shouldn't be, missing time or missing distance, disoriented and easy for someone worse to find first.

Thread: The Incident, Still Loose

Deliberately open, consistent with chapter 01's framing - no fixed agenda, revels in chaos, wearing some new host somewhere. Where it is and what it's doing next is GM's call in the moment, not decided here. If branch B above is chosen, this thread and Marcus's thread merge into one.

Thread: The Foundation Closes In

The season's escalation shape: Case Officer Broussard's investigation (paperwork, quiet questions, no direct contact) as the early pressure, Special Agent Chastain's personal involvement as the mid-season turn - she starts as an observer, then a conversation, then eventually a confrontation - building toward a real Foundation response once the party's visibility crosses some threshold. No fixed session count; GM's call based on how the table's actually playing it.

Thread: Gulf Coast Division & Site 46

L-???-840-0046 (reserved) - the individual number is allocated and the origin field is not, because nobody has written down which state the site is in. Allocated numbers are never reissued, so 0046 is spent whether or not the file ever gets filled in.

The burned incident log from Case File LA-1147 left two lines deliberately unreadable: a "Gulf Coast breach" and Site 46 reporting activity that's "roughly tripled." Both stay genuinely unresolved here - a deliberate unpaid-off hook for later, not a mystery with a secret answer already decided. Build the payoff whenever the season actually needs one.

Decided vs. Genuinely Open

  • DecidedThe Foundation's general escalation shape (Broussard, then Chastain, then a full response).
  • OpenMarcus's fate - pick a branch above, or leave it ambiguous.
  • OpenThe Incident's current host and destination.
  • OpenWhat Site 46's activity spike actually is.
  • OpenWhat happens if the party doesn't go looking for Marcus at all - a real alternate season shape, not a railroad.
