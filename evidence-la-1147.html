Subject Designation: LA-1147

Containment Class: NOMAD — non-corporeal, requires a living host, cannot be conventionally contained without a host-compatible vessel or an active suppression field.
Description: Subject is a non-corporeal intelligence capable of assuming complete control of a living host without physiological trace. Subject exhibits no consistent behavioral pattern between hosts; motive and objective appear to derive from host psychology rather than an intrinsic agenda. Subject has demonstrated the ability to manifest localized reality-distortion effects through host action. Frequency and scale not yet fully quantified.
Containment History: Subject was successfully contained following a prior incident (see cross-reference, restricted). Subject was held in suppressed stasis via Anchor-pattern containment field pending transfer to long-term storage.
Incident Log — Transport Failure: Subject escaped containment during transport between facilities. Preliminary review indicates transport checklist Section 4 (Anchor Field Integrity Verification) was not completed prior to departure. Subject is presumed to have exited containment during the transport window. Personnel responsible have been remanded to ██████████.
Current Status: Subject has been tracked to New Orleans, LA. Confirmed current host: Marcus ████████. Host is a private citizen with no prior Foundation contact. Subject's behavior in this host suggests a retaliatory motive against Foundation personnel. Recommend immediate extraction team deployment. Anchor-pattern extraction equipment authorized for this operation — see item requisition, this file.
Threat Assessment: Subject itself poses no direct physical threat (non-corporeal, cannot act without a host). Risk is entirely a function of host capability and subject's demonstrated retaliatory intent. Host is believed to be an unwilling vessel — treat as Class-2 hostile for the duration of this operation, without prejudice.
