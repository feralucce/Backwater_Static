Scorched Napkin

Recovered On Scene · Cocktail Napkin, Scorched

A cheap paper cocktail napkin, curled black along one edge like it was held too close to a candle and pulled back too late. The handwriting is cramped, pressed hard enough to tear the paper in places — whoever wrote this went over some words two and three times.
