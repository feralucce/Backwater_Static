Site 23, Floor Plans

L-ORL-840-0023

Companion · Hex Overlay · 1 in = 1 m
Everything on this page is spoilers

Real, to-scale floor plans for Site 23 - one above-ground building, two stories, tucked into a working stretch of the docks near Poland Avenue Wharf. Distinct from the city-level city map, this is what's actually inside. Companion to The Organization.

Built to scale, hex overlay. Unlike Repository 23 (a real decommissioned property the campaign repurposes), Site 23 isn't a public building - it's Foundation infrastructure grafted into an active, working section of the wharf, deliberately unremarkable from outside. The footprint (20 m x 12 m per floor) and every room inside it are original GM content, sized to plausibly disappear among real dock/terminal-operations buildings. Every diagram below is set to true physical inches (viewBox = inches), 1 hex = 1 m - printing at 100% scale (not "fit to page") gives real 1 inch = 1 meter.
Access note. Floors 1 and 2 are reachable by anyone who talks or badges their way in the front door. The basement is not - it's elevator-only, badge-gated from the utility closet on Floor 1, and the emergency stair shaft that would otherwise reach it is sealed and alarmed. Nobody stumbles into the basement by accident.

Ground Floor · Public-Facing

Floor 1

20 m x 12 m footprint - the building's cover story, and its only entrances from the street and the yard
1-1 · Lobby / Reception

The building's actual front door, off the wharf road. A reception counter, a few waiting chairs - exactly what a small terminal-operations office should look like, because that's what it's built to be mistaken for.

1-2 · Security Office

Controls badge access to everything past the lobby - the corridor, the utility closet, and eventually the elevator. Watches both the yard cameras and the internal halls from here.

1-3 · Corridor

Plain hallway connecting the front-of-house rooms to the back offices and the loading bay. Nothing to see, which is the point.

1-4 · Dockmaster's Office

Real work happens here, not just cover - vessel schedules, container manifests, the actual paperwork of a working wharf. Whoever holds this job may not even know what's under their feet.

1-5 · Break Room

Mundane. Coffee, a table, four chairs - the one room in the building that could belong to any office anywhere.

1-6 · Utility Closet

Unmarked from the corridor side - a breaker panel bolted to the door is the only signage. The elevator inside only answers to a badge with the right clearance, and it only goes one place.

1-7 · Loading Bay

The building's real working entrance, off the yard - crates and personnel move through here, not the lobby. Stairs up to Floor 2 tucked in the corner.

The floor a stranger sees, and the floor most of Site 23's own day-to-day staff never leave. The napkin found at The Screen names this stretch of docks directly - Poland Ave, containers stacked high enough to hide the fence behind them - but says nothing about what's underneath.

Upper Floor · Staff Only

Floor 2

Same 20 m x 12 m footprint - reached only by the stairs in the loading bay below
2-1 · Site Director's Office

The only room in the building with a real window - and it faces the yard, not the street. Better furniture than anywhere else up here.

2-2 · Records / Archive

Filing cabinets and a locked server rack - the local paper trail, personnel files and case logs for Site 23's own staff and caseload. Not the same catalog as Repository 23's; that one's a different building entirely.

2-3 · Corridor

Dead-ends at the Armory's own door rather than running its full length - the one room up here that doesn't open directly onto the hall.

2-4 · Surveillance Room

A bank of monitors covering the docks outside and every camera inside, including Floor 1's lobby and loading bay. Whoever's on this shift sees everyone who comes and goes.

2-5 · Briefing Room

Where Field & Tactical and the Bayou Reapers get their tasking before a run. A plain table, nothing on the walls that wouldn't fit a shipping company.

2-6 · Armory

A narrow, secure strip running the full depth of the building, its own single door off the corridor. Racked long arms and gear for Site 23's Field & Tactical roster - see the Adversary Index.

2-7 · Stairwell & Break Nook

Stairs down to the loading bay, plus a small kitchenette - staff who work this floor rarely have a reason to go down to the lobby at all.

The building's actual operational floor - everyone who works Site 23's real caseload passes through here, and almost none of them have ever seen what's below the utility closet downstairs.

Restricted · Badge Access Only · Elevator Only

Basement

Same 20 m x 12 m footprint - no stairs reach it; the emergency shaft above is sealed and alarmed
B-1 · Elevator Vestibule

Where the elevator actually opens - a blast door between the car and the rest of the level, badged separately from the elevator call itself. Two checks, not one.

B-2 · Suppression Field Generator

Feeds the Anchor-pattern field to every cell down here. A low hum, felt in the floor more than heard, running whether or not a cell is currently occupied - this is what the napkin's "green room that hummed all night" was describing.

B-3 · Containment Corridor

Every cell door and the guard post both open onto it. No windows, no distractions - just a long hallway under sodium light.

B-4 · Cell 1 (empty)

LA-1147's cell before the transport failure - still green-lit, still humming faintly on standby, nobody's gotten around to powering it fully down. This is the room the napkin remembers.

B-4 · Cell 2 (LA-0892)

Currently occupied - one of the active regional designations named in the burned incident log recovered from The Screen (see Case File LA-1147). What's actually inside is GM's call.

B-4 · Cells 3 & 4 (empty)

Unoccupied, held in reserve. Site 23's real ongoing caseload - the Rougarou, the Cypress Coiler, the Backstreet Choir, and the rest of the Loose Anomalies - is still out in the city, not down here; these cells are for whatever gets caught next.

B-5 · Guard Post

A single desk that sees every cell door and both directions of the corridor at once. Never left unattended while a cell is occupied.

B-6 · Medical / Exam

An exam table and supply shelving - for a subject, a host, or Foundation personnel who came off a bad extraction. If Marcus's body ends up in Site 23's custody later this season (see Campaign Arc & Threads), this is the most likely room for him.

B-7 · Evidence & Effects Lockup

Tagged personal effects, bagged and shelved by case number - whatever a subject or a host had on them when the Foundation caught up to them.

This is where the ledger page's "rows of us in the dark down there behind the chainlink" actually is - though the page found it secondhand, from someone who never got past the elevator vestibule. Most of Site 23's own staff have never been down here either.
